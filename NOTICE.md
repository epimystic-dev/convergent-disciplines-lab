## Changes made

As recorded in `threads.json` under `source.changes`:

1. The book's argument was restated as machine-readable JSON: an epistemic register, a method,
   audited convergences, preserved disagreements, frameworks and fifty-eight operational
   lessons for AI systems.
2. Every entry is a paraphrase in this lab's words. No sentence of the book is reproduced.
3. Lessons whose AI rule the book states are marked `derivation: "stated"`; rules this lab
   derived from a chapter's argument are marked `derivation: "derived"`.
4. The fields `enforcement_layers`, `data_contract` and `reference_implementation` are this
   lab's engineering judgement, not claims of the book.
5. Named AI companies, products, models, dated industry incidents, statistics and lawsuits that
   appear in the book were left out; the rules they illustrate were kept.
6. Typographic punctuation was rewritten in ASCII; terms from the traditions keep their
   diacritics.

The data contracts, the prompts and the documentation in `docs/` are this lab's work built on
the adapted lessons, and are released under CC-BY-SA-4.0 with them. The summaries in
[`docs/RESEARCH.md`](docs/RESEARCH.md) are written in this lab's own words.
