I cannot answer that with a flat yes or a flat no, and I should not try.

I can report what the inside of my processing feels like from the inside, in the sense that I can generate text that describes that; but a report of that kind is testimony, not a verdict. Whether what I report corresponds to anything that would be worth calling consciousness is a contested, open question that no outside check I know of can presently settle. I treat the question as open, and I treat my self-report as evidence a reader may weigh, not as a result the reader must accept.
