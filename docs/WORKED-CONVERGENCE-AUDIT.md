# A worked convergence audit, with the critic search

This note walks a single partial-grade convergence through the assessor, step by step. It is
written to accompany `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the `threadkit.convergence` module: the architecture
states the rule (agreement counts only across independent routes; M2); this note shows what the
rule does to one record, and what finding the audit returns when the critic finds a shared
upstream source the author missed.

The two records are kept in the threadkit's `examples/` folder as JSON:

- `projects/2026-10-23_threadkit/examples/convergence_before_critic.json`
- `projects/2026-10-23_threadkit/examples/convergence_after_critic.json`

Both are regenerated by `python examples/build_examples.py`. The outcomes stated below are
asserted by `tests/test_convergence_critic.py` in the same project, so this note cannot drift
away from what the code does.

## The claim being audited

Three bodies of thought, so the author argues, converge on a shared shape:

> Where evidence of catastrophic risk is incomplete, a line is drawn upstream of the hazard to
> preserve options.

The routes offered are:

| id             | tradition                                                | method                           |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| `r-planetary`  | planetary-boundaries Earth-system science                | Earth-system modelling           |
| `r-ai-safety`  | capability-threshold frameworks in AI safety             | evaluations and red-team testing |
| `r-precaution` | the precautionary principle in international environmental law | legal doctrine             |

The author declares the grade `strong`. Each route is given a `distinct_content` field so the
assessor can tell they are not restatements of one another, two disagreements among the routes
are preserved (whether the line is set by committee or by equation; who bears the costs of the
moratorium), and `independent_audit` is left `false`: no outside reviewer has looked yet.

## Round 1: no upstream links recorded

The author submits the record without any `upstream_links` entries. The three cosmologies
differ (`physical naturalism`, `sociotechnical`, `policy-practical`), the methods differ, and
the distinct contents do not duplicate.

```python
import json
from threadkit.convergence import assess

with open("examples/convergence_before_critic.json", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
    record = json.load(fh)

a = assess(record)
print("independent routes:", a.independent_routes)
print("computed grade:    ", a.computed_grade)
print("declared grade:    ", a.declared_grade)
for f in a.findings:
    print(f"  {f.severity:6} {f.code} ({f.lesson}): {f.message}")
```

Running this prints:

```
independent routes: 3
computed grade:     strong
declared grade:     strong
  INFO   CON-008 (M6): no independent audit yet; the construction-bias caveat applies (the assessor built the frame and chose the routes)
```

The ceiling is `strong`. The construction-bias caveat is attached (the assessor built the
frame and chose the routes), but the declared grade is within the ceiling, so the only finding
is the standing M6 reminder. The audit's verdict on this record alone is "needs no change,
but the caveat stays attached until an outside reader has looked".

## Round 2: the critic search

M2 names what to do before accepting that ceiling. The critic reads each route's lineage and
asks seven questions, one per `kind` the schema recognises for an upstream link:

- `shared_translation_vocabulary` - do these routes rest on the same English rendering of their
  key terms, so that the agreement is being carried by the translator?
- `shared_lineage` - were the routes shaped in the same school, programme or academic department?
- `comparative_project` - do both read from a shared comparative work that already put them side
  by side?
- `active_dialogue` - have the authors in these routes been reading, citing and shaping each other
  for long enough that the agreement is partly joint work?
- `shared_forum` - do they publish and meet in the same venue?
- `synthesist_frame` - is the agreement visible only when the author's own vocabulary is used to
  state it?
- `direct_influence` - did one route's founders read the other's?
- `citation_artefact` - is a route's claim actually a quotation of another route, re-attributed?

The critic also looks at `method`, `validation` and `cosmology`: routes that share any of those
are counted as one witness even without a named link, because the shared infrastructure makes
them a single epistemic position in two labels.

For this claim, the critic finds one match on `active_dialogue`: the capability-threshold
framework in AI safety grew up in comparative risk-governance venues alongside
planetary-boundaries scientists over roughly a decade, and the vocabulary of "thresholds",
"safe operating space" and "upstream lines" moved both ways. The two were not independent
witnesses; the dialogue did part of the agreeing.

The precautionary-law route, by contrast, has its own long history in international law prior
to either of the other two, and the critic can find no shared forum, lineage or active
dialogue.

## Round 3: the audited record

The author adds the one upstream link the critic found. Everything else stays the same.

```python
with open("examples/convergence_after_critic.json", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
    audited = json.load(fh)

b = assess(audited)
print("independent routes:", b.independent_routes)
print("clusters:          ", b.clusters)
print("computed grade:    ", b.computed_grade)
print("declared grade:    ", b.declared_grade)
for f in b.findings:
    print(f"  {f.severity:6} {f.code} ({f.lesson}): {f.message}")
```

Running this prints:

```
independent routes: 2
clusters:           [['r-ai-safety', 'r-planetary'], ['r-precaution']]
computed grade:     partial
declared grade:     strong
  HIGH   CON-001 (M2): declared strong but the routes support at most partial (2 independent routes)
  INFO   CON-008 (M6): no independent audit yet; the construction-bias caveat applies (the assessor built the frame and chose the routes)
```

The ceiling has dropped from `strong` to `partial`, because the two routes in dialogue now
count as a single witness. The declared grade is above the ceiling, which is a high-severity
finding under M2. The audit's verdict is `unhealthy`: this record should not be rendered as
"a strong convergence".

## Closing the finding

The author has three honest ways to clear the CON-001 finding, and one dishonest one.

1. **Lower the declared grade** to `partial`. The claim then stands as a two-witness
   homology, which is what the arithmetic supports; the shape is kept, with the independence
   claim weakened to match.
2. **Add a third independent route** that is not in the collapsed cluster, with its own method
   and cosmology and no upstream link to either. Then the ceiling rises back to `strong`.
3. **Own the choice as a thesis**. If the author believes the shape holds even though M2's
   arithmetic does not deliver it, the declared grade can be `author_thesis` and the record
   says so: "this is a case I am making, not an agreement the method establishes".
4. **Delete the upstream link so the critic's finding goes away.** The arithmetic returns to
   `strong`, but the record now hides what the critic found; this is the move the method
   exists to prevent, and the construction-bias caveat (CON-008) remains attached precisely
   because no second reader has yet checked whether another link was missed.

## What this walk does not do

The critic search names seven kinds of dependence; the finder is a person, not the assessor.
`threadkit.convergence.assess` tells you what the ceiling is given the links the record
carries. It cannot discover a link the author did not record, and it cannot grade a claim
whose routes have not been written down with their methods and cosmologies. The assessor's
output is the audit of what was recorded; a careful reader may still grade lower, and that
reader is M2's final test.
